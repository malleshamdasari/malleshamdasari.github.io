I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Northeastern University. I direct the Spatial Intelligence Research Group (SINRG).

Email: m.dasari@northeastern.edu   •   Curriculum Vitae (PDF)

My research advances the science and engineering of Spatial Intelligence, which spans several interconnected areas of computing , AI and human-centered technologies:

  • 4D Geometry, World Models, and AI Data Infrastructure

    Representation, capture, compression, and streaming of dynamic 4D data including point clouds, meshes, Gaussian splats, volumetric 6-DoF videos, worldfields, and networked world models.

  • Human, Machine, AI Interactions

    Interfaces and visual systems that enable humans, computers, robots, AI models to interact with spatial data through AR/VR platforms, collaborative environments, and interactive visual representations of complex environments.

  • Cognitive and Intelligent Wearable Systems

    Neural sensing on smart glasses, brain--computer interfaces (BCIs), decoding human attention, intent, and neural activity, adaptive human–AI systems.

  • Education, Learning, and Workforce Training

    Using spatial AI and AR/VR technologies to transform education, training, and skill development through experiential learning systems and collaborative digital environments.

My work has been supported by the U.S. National Science Foundation (NSF), National Security Agency (NSA), and Department of Defense (DoD). I received the NSF CAREER Award (2026) and several best paper and demo awards: including the Best Paper Award Honorable Mention at IEEE Mixed and Augmented Reality (ISMAR) conference (2026), the Best Reproducible Paper Award at ACM Multimedia Systems (MMSys) conference (2025), the Best Demo Award at ACM HotMobile conference (2025), the Innovation Award from the AI-RAN Alliance (2025), the Best Demo Award at DARPA SRC Symposium (2023), and the Best Paper Award at IEEE Multimedia conference (2021).

Teaching

EECE 5512: Introduction to Spatial Intelligence
Spring 2027, Fall 2025, Fall 2024, Spring 2024: Northeastern University

An interdisciplinary course covering emerging topics from immersive media, HCI, computer vision, graphics, and computer networks. In addition to lectures, the course includes experiential sessions with a variety of state-of-the-art spatial computers like AR/VR headsets and smart glasses.

  • Fundamentals: computational and human spatial intelligence
  • Fundamentals: visual media, spatial computers, legacy computers
  • Fundamentals: information, entropy, digital world representations, and the Internet
  • 2D, flat/stereo 360, 3D and volumetric videos: RGB-D, point cloud, mesh, NeRF
  • Monocular, stereoscopic, and multiview videos, and world models
  • Acquiring immersive content for network delivery
  • Compression for RGB, depth, point clouds, and mesh sequences
  • Streaming fundamentals: stored, live, and interactive protocols
  • Streaming immersive content: videos, point clouds, meshes, holograms, spaces
  • Visual and wireless sensing for human motion tracking
  • Building immersive systems such as 3D telepresence and spatial web applications
  • Tracking eyes, hands, face, head, body; outside-in and inside-out systems
  • ARKit, Unity, Open3D, Open4D, Pytorch3D, and networked AR/VR platforms
